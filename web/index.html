Encrypted

Three layers of protection

Everything is encrypted on your device: full-disk encryption, a password-protected database, and a second layer on sensitive fields. Your health data stays encrypted even if someone bypasses the app.

Local-first

Your device, not our server

Data lives on your device by default. Optional sync is opt-in per data type. You choose what moves and where it goes. Nothing is shared automatically.

No tracking

Nothing phones home

The app has no analytics, crash reporters, or ad networks. It doesn't send data anywhere you didn't ask it to. What you log stays on your device.
