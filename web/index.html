Encrypted

Three layers of protection

Every entry is encrypted at rest — device encryption, an SQLCipher-encrypted database, and per-field AES-256-GCM on sensitive columns. Your health data is ciphertext, even if someone bypasses the app.

Local-first

Your phone, not our server

Data lives on your device by default. Optional sync is opt-in per data type — you choose what moves and where it goes. Nothing is shared automatically.

No tracking

Zero third-party SDKs

No analytics. No crash reporters. No ad networks. The app doesn't phone home. What you log stays between you and your device.
