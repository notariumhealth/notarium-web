Community poll

Help pick the logo.

Notarium is a private, local-first app for people tracking their health and documenting chronic illness, along with the workplace-accommodation process that often comes with it. The mark should read as quiet and exact. It protects the person and avoids clinical or hype registers.

Voting has closed. The nine concepts that were on the ballot are below.

The nine concepts

Result: Logo 6

Logo 6 won. It led on first-choice votes (7 of 18), led on weighted ranking, appeared on more ballots than any other mark, and was the only concept nobody voted against. 13 of 18 said they would install an app with their top pick as its icon.

Thank you to everyone who ranked the concepts. Voting is closed and the gallery above stays up as a record of what was on the ballot. The mark still has to clear a trademark and emblem review before it ships, so it may yet change; if it does, that will be said here.
