# Founding board

Seat 6 drives the case that the org can raise real money against a specific mission.

This is one of the founding board's five function seats. I am recruiting an external director to fill it.

## Who I am looking for

A nonprofit development professional with one or more of:

- Grant writing (federal, foundation, or corporate)
- Individual donor cultivation and major-gift work
- Nonprofit development strategy at a small org (under $500K annual budget)
- Health-focused foundation relationships (for example the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, RWJF, or patient-community foundations)

## Why this seat matters

The 501(c)(3) formation opens grant channels that the LLC could not access. Year one has to demonstrate the org can raise real money against a specific mission. This seat drives that.

## What you would do

- Serve on the Development Committee, and chair it once we can staff it
- Contribute to grant application drafting, especially the case-for-support and theory-of-change sections
- Advise on the individual-donor program design
- Identify prospective foundation funders whose mission matches Notarium's
- Introduce the org to donors and grantmakers in your network where appropriate

## Time commitment

The base of four hours per month, higher during grant deadlines.

## What you get

- A seat on a board where your work directly shapes whether the org can fund the mission
- Public credit as a founding director
- A contribution to a small, mission-locked org where every grant dollar goes to program, not overhead layers
- A working relationship with a founder who ships code and does not need to be told what nonprofit runway means

## How to express interest

Email me at sophia@notarium.health and name this seat. I will send a forty-five minute intro call invitation and answer any questions before either of us commits. A call is not a commitment to serve. If it feels right, we set a start date after the 501(c)(3) formation completes.

Referrals are especially welcome for this seat.

## About Notarium

Notarium is a private, local-first Android app for people managing chronic illness or disability while navigating workplace leave and accommodations. It helps users log symptoms, track medications, record flare-ups, and preserve documentation that holds up under HR review or an EEOC charge. Everything stays on the user's device by default, encrypted at the file system, at the database, and per field for the most sensitive columns. Any upstream sync is opt-in per data category, never global. The source is public under AGPL-3.0.
