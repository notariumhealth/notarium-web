# Founding board

Seat 2 keeps Notarium honest about what its evidence exports can and cannot claim, and carries the 501(c)(3) through formation and ongoing compliance.

This is one of the founding board's five function seats. I am recruiting an external director to fill it.

## Who I am looking for

An attorney, paralegal, or governance professional with one or more of these backgrounds:

- 501(c)(3) nonprofit formation, compliance, or ongoing governance
- Disability rights law (ADA, FMLA, state leave law)
- Health data privacy (HIPAA, state health privacy laws, or GDPR if we ever serve international users)
- Employment law with an accommodation practice

## Why this seat matters

Notarium's evidence exports have to hold up under HR review, EEOC charge review, and attorney review. A director with legal fluency in one of these areas keeps the product honest about what it can and cannot claim. The 501(c)(3) formation and ongoing compliance, including annual filings, unrelated business income analysis, and private benefit review, also draw on this seat.

## What you would do

- Review bylaws revisions before board votes
- Chair the Governance Committee once we have three or more seats to staff it
- Review any user-facing legal language (Terms, Privacy Policy, evidence-export disclaimers) before public release
- Represent the board's legal perspective in vendor and partner negotiations (D&O renewals, hosting terms, grant contracts)
- Serve as the go-to for founder questions on legal risk

## Time commitment

The base of four hours per month, plus review turns around bylaws and policy documents.

## What you get

- Public credit as a founding director of a mission-locked FOSS health nonprofit
- A vote on how the product's evidence framing is scoped
- A working relationship with a security-focused founder who does not need hand-holding on infosec but does need legal counsel on the accommodation-record use case
- A contribution to a moat around a real gap in the market

## How to express interest

Email me at sophia@notarium.health and name this seat. I will send a forty-five minute intro call invitation and answer any questions before either of us commits. A call is not a commitment to serve. If it feels right, we set a start date after the 501(c)(3) formation completes.

If this is not you but you know someone who might fit, referrals are welcome.

## About Notarium

Notarium is a private, local-first Android app for people managing chronic illness or disability while navigating workplace leave and accommodations. It helps users log symptoms, track medications, record flare-ups, and preserve documentation that holds up under HR review or an EEOC charge. Everything stays on the user's device by default, encrypted at the file system, at the database, and per field for the most sensitive columns. Any upstream sync is opt-in per data category, never global. The source is public under AGPL-3.0.
