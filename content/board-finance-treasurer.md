# Founding board

Seat 3 holds the board's confidence that money is being handled correctly, from the first budget through Form 990.

This is one of the founding board's five function seats. I am recruiting an external director to serve as Treasurer.

## Who I am looking for

A CPA, nonprofit finance officer, or professional with treasurer experience at a 501(c)(3). Public accountant experience is preferred but not required if you have run the finance function at a small nonprofit before.

## Why this seat matters

The 501(c)(3) has ongoing financial reporting obligations: Form 990, state charitable solicitation registrations, and sales-tax exemptions per state. Grant applications need clean financials. Donors and grantmakers do due diligence on Form 990 filings. This seat holds the board's confidence that money is being handled correctly.

## What you would do

- Serve as Treasurer officer of the board
- Chair the Audit Committee once we can staff it
- Present quarterly financials at board meetings
- Coordinate with the outside CPA on Form 990 preparation
- Review the annual budget draft before board approval
- Authorize disbursements above a threshold set in the bylaws

## Time commitment

The base of four hours per month, spiking to eight or ten hours in the two months around Form 990 filing.

## What you get

- Public credit as founding Treasurer of a small but publicly visible FOSS health nonprofit
- A seat on a working board where your expertise is directly load-bearing, not decorative
- A working relationship with a founder who reads P&Ls and does not want financial surprises
- A contribution to a small org where good financial hygiene compounds fast

## How to express interest

Email me at sophia@notarium.health and name this seat. I will send a forty-five minute intro call invitation and answer any questions before either of us commits. A call is not a commitment to serve. If it feels right, we set a start date after the 501(c)(3) formation completes.

If this is not you but you know someone who might fit, referrals are welcome.

## About Notarium

Notarium is a private, local-first Android app for people managing chronic illness or disability while navigating workplace leave and accommodations. It helps users log symptoms, track medications, record flare-ups, and preserve documentation that holds up under HR review or an EEOC charge. Everything stays on the user's device by default, encrypted at the file system, at the database, and per field for the most sensitive columns. Any upstream sync is opt-in per data category, never global. The source is public under AGPL-3.0.
