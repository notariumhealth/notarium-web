# Account conduct

Notarium posts from a small number of public accounts, currently on Mastodon and Bluesky. This page states what those accounts will and will not do, so anyone deciding whether to follow one can hold it to something written down.

The accounts are operated by Sophia Daw, the project maintainer. Nothing is scheduled and nothing is automated; every post is written by hand. If anyone else is given posting access, reading this page and the rules of the host server is a condition of getting it, not an expectation afterwards.

Conduct in the repository and the issue tracker is covered by the [Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct) instead.

## What the accounts are for

- Release notes and what changed in the app
- Security disclosures and their fixes
- Accessibility and design notes, including decisions that turned out badly
- Talking with other free software, health, and disability projects

They are not a marketing channel. A release gets announced once. If there is nothing new to say, nothing gets posted.

## No patient information

The accounts do not post identifiable patient information, health data, or user stories, including anonymized ones, without explicit written consent from the person involved.

Disclosing a health condition carries real employment and insurance consequences for the person disclosing it, and no amount of engagement is worth handing someone that risk. The same rule covers screenshots: anything published from the app shows synthetic data, not a real person's log.

## No medical or legal advice

Notarium helps a person document their own situation. It does not tell them what a diagnosis means or what their rights are, and neither do these accounts. Questions of that kind get pointed toward a clinician, an attorney, or the agency that actually handles them.

## Charged topics

Disability rights, healthcare access, and workplace accommodation are politically contested in the United States, and this project sits inside that. The accounts speak factually about what the software does and about how the accommodation process is documented. They stay out of adjacent political fights that do not concern the project.

If a thread stops being useful, the account disengages rather than escalating. It does not pile on, and it does not use its follower count as a weapon in someone else's argument.

## The rules of the host server

Each account also follows the rules of the server it posts from. Where a host's rules are stricter than this page, the host's rules win.

On Hachyderm that means the server rules, the Specialized Account Expectations for accounts like this one, and the guidance on charged topics. Moderators there are volunteers, and an account that needs moderating has already failed at self-moderation. That is the standard this page is meant to hold.

## Reporting

If one of these accounts breaks something written here, write to [conduct@notarium.health](mailto:conduct@notarium.health).

Reporting to the moderators of the host server is always an option too, and it is the better one if the complaint is about us. That route does not depend on the project acting in good faith.
