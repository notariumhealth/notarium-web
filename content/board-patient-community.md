# Founding board

Seat 4 keeps the board honest about whether product decisions actually help people, not just power users like the founder.

This is one of the founding board's five function seats. I am recruiting an external director to fill it.

## Who I am looking for

A person with direct patient advocacy experience in one or more of:

- Chronic illness (POTS, hEDS, long COVID, ME/CFS, autoimmune conditions, chronic pain)
- Disability rights organizing
- Patient-led research or patient community moderation
- ADA accommodation navigation as a patient, an employee, or a professional

## Why this seat matters

Notarium exists because manual health tracking is broken for people whose data lives inside their bodies, not on a smartwatch. This seat keeps the board honest about whether product decisions actually help people. Patient voice on the board is also table stakes for patient-community trust and for grantmakers who fund patient-centered work.

## What you would do

- Serve as the board's reference point when product decisions touch UX, accessibility, or symptom-vocabulary questions
- Review the roadmap once per year and flag anything that drifts away from patient utility
- Represent the board at patient community events when interest and capacity allow
- Contribute to grant applications where a patient-perspective narrative strengthens the case
- Advise on any research collaboration proposal that would use Notarium data

## Independence

This seat should not be a friend of the founder. If a patient-community connection surfaces someone qualified whom I already know, we route the vetting through a different director so the independence requirement holds.

## Time commitment

The base of four hours per month. No committee chair expectation unless you want it.

## What you get

- A vote on how a patient-facing product gets built and framed
- A contribution to a project run by a founder who lives inside the use case rather than interpreting it from outside
- Public credit as a founding director of a FOSS health nonprofit

## How to express interest

Email me at sophia@notarium.health and name this seat. I will send a forty-five minute intro call invitation and answer any questions before either of us commits. A call is not a commitment to serve. If it feels right, we set a start date after the 501(c)(3) formation completes.

Referrals are especially welcome for this seat.

## About Notarium

Notarium is a private, local-first Android app for people managing chronic illness or disability while navigating workplace leave and accommodations. It helps users log symptoms, track medications, record flare-ups, and preserve documentation that holds up under HR review or an EEOC charge. Everything stays on the user's device by default, encrypted at the file system, at the database, and per field for the most sensitive columns. Any upstream sync is opt-in per data category, never global. The source is public under AGPL-3.0.
