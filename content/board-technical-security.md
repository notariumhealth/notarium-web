# Founding board

Seat 5 is peer review for Notarium's security claims, held by someone with no professional debt to the founder.

This is one of the founding board's five function seats. I am recruiting an external director to fill it, under a hard independence constraint.

## Who I am looking for

A senior technologist with one or more of:

- Information security (application security, cryptography, threat modeling)
- Health IT (FHIR, HL7, health data interoperability, HIPAA implementation)
- FOSS project governance or maintainership
- Mobile platform security (Android or iOS)

## Why this seat matters

Notarium makes strong security claims: three-layer encryption, opt-in-per-row sync, no third-party SDKs, no analytics. A director with the technical background to sanity-check those claims protects the org from asserting things the code cannot back. Grantmakers and patient communities want to see technical review that is not the founder's own.

## Independence requirement

This seat cannot be a current or former direct professional connection of mine. No coworkers, no former colleagues, no professional-network first-degree relationships. This is a hard constraint, because the seat's job is peer review, and peer review from someone with a professional debt to me is not peer review.

## What you would do

- Serve as the board's technical reviewer for architecture, threat model, and security claims
- Review the security posture annually and flag anything that has drifted
- Advise on any security incident that reaches the board (breach, disclosure, coordinated vulnerability response)
- Review vendor security assessments when the org onboards a new vendor with data access
- Contribute technical credibility to grant applications where the security posture is a selling point

## Time commitment

The base of four hours per month. Higher during any security incident or major architecture change.

## What you get

- A contribution to a rare kind of project: a health-data app that took security seriously from day one
- A seat on a board where your technical judgment is load-bearing on the mission
- Public credit as a founding director
- A working relationship with a founder who is a peer, not a customer

## How to express interest

Email me at sophia@notarium.health and name this seat. I will send a forty-five minute intro call invitation and answer any questions before either of us commits. A call is not a commitment to serve. If it feels right, we set a start date after the 501(c)(3) formation completes.

If this is not you but you know someone who might fit, referrals are welcome, respecting the independence constraint.

## About Notarium

Notarium is a private, local-first Android app for people managing chronic illness or disability while navigating workplace leave and accommodations. It helps users log symptoms, track medications, record flare-ups, and preserve documentation that holds up under HR review or an EEOC charge. Everything stays on the user's device by default, encrypted at the file system, at the database, and per field for the most sensitive columns. Any upstream sync is opt-in per data category, never global. The source is public under AGPL-3.0.
